What the label is for

The clothing funds the mission

NVR Supply sells heavyweight pieces. NVR Foundation funds free education. The clothing pays for the school, and the school gives the clothing its meaning. Neither works alone — that's the moat.

Three layers

Foundation holds the mission and the money. Supply holds the clothing and the objects. Academy holds the free coding school.

The three rules

  1. Never use fake urgency or discount spam.
  2. Never post content you wouldn't wear.
  3. Never ship something that doesn't fund the mission.
