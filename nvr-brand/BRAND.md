# NVR — Brand Bible

## Meaning
**NVR = "NEVER."** Not a word. A stance.
Expansion: **Never Evolve, Never Rest** — the internal meaning behind the three letters.

## Positioning line
> **Never stop.**
> NVR is a learning organization that funds free education — and the clothing label built to wear it.

## Architecture
| Layer | Name | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Organization | **NVR Foundation** | Mission, funding, community |
| Product | **NVR Supply** | Clothing and objects |
| Program | **NVR Academy** | Free coding school (this site) |

The clothing funds the mission. The mission gives the clothing meaning. Neither works alone — that's the moat.

## Voice
Short. Certain. Never salesy.
- ✅ "Never stop." / "Built for the ones who don't." / "Wear the mission."
- ❌ "Shop now! Limited stock!! Buy your dream life!"

## Logo
- Wordmark: **NVR** — heavy geometric sans, wide tracking, all caps
- Mark: brain-as-planet with an orbital ring (`logo-mark.svg`)
- Lockup: mark left, wordmark right (`logo.svg`)
- Minimum size: 24px height. Never stretch. Never add effects.
- Clear space: height of the "N" on all sides.

## Color
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Void | `#040817` | Primary background |
| Bone | `#e6ecff` | Type |
| Volt | `#c6f24e` | Signal collection, CTAs |
| Ultraviolet | `#a78bfa` | Orbit collection, gradients |
| Cyan | `#7dd3fc` | Links, data |

Rule: void background + ONE accent per piece. Never two accents together.

## Type
- Headlines: Space Grotesk / any geometric grotesk, ALL CAPS, tight tracking
- Body: Inter, sentence case
- Data/labels: monospace, uppercase, wide tracking (this is the "techwear" tell)

## Garment direction
- Heavyweight 240–320gsm cotton, boxy, drop shoulder
- Oversized back print (single statement, 1 line max)
- Small chest or sleeve wordmark
- Blank inside + outside labels both branded
- Every garment has an NVR tag: "This funded a learner's first course."

## Community tiers
| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Guest** | Learning for free (Academy) |
| **Member** | Gets early drops + Academy forever |

## The 3 rules
1. Never use fake urgency or discount spam.
2. Never post content you wouldn't wear.
3. Never ship something that doesn't fund the mission.
