Privacy

How LearnStack looks after your data

LearnStack needs to remember where you got to. It does not need to know who you are. This page explains exactly what is saved.

What is saved

  • Which lessons you have started and finished.
  • Your best quiz score and how many times you tried.
  • The date of your next review for a finished lesson.
  • Your display settings: theme, easy-read mode, and an optional nickname.
  • Whether you felt ready before a quiz. This is private, it is not a score, and no one else sees it.

What is not saved

  • Your name, email address, age, school, or home town.
  • Photos, voice recordings, or free text. There is no chat and nowhere to type a message.
  • Advertising identifiers or third-party analytics. There are no ads and no trackers.
  • Your answers to the tracker's practice questions. LearnStack keeps its own separate data.

Where it is saved

First, in this browser on this device. If the adult who set it up turned on saving to the cloud, a copy is also kept in a private database row that only this browser can read. That row has no name and no email attached to it.

Who can see it

You, on this device. Nobody else has a learner account, a class list, or a dashboard showing your scores. LearnStack has no leaderboard and no public profile.

How to delete it

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Choose Delete my LearnStack data.
  3. Read the confirmation, then confirm.

This removes your progress, scores, review dates, nickname, and display settings from this browser. If a cloud copy exists, it is removed too. If you are offline at the time, the cloud copy is removed the next time you are online. Deletion cannot be undone.

If something worries you

Speak to the adult who set up LearnStack for you. This page does not collect a contact address, and there is no public form to fill in.

LearnStack is a learning aid. It is not an exam board and does not give certificates or official results.
