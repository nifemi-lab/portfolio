About NVR Coding 🧠

NVR Coding is a psychology-first coding school that runs entirely in your browser. It exists because most courses dump information at you — ours is built around how brains actually learn:

🧩 Chunking

Lessons are 2–4 minute micro-steps, never walls of text.

🎯 Active recall

Quizzes and write-it steps force you to retrieve, not just read.

🔁 Spaced repetition

The Review page resurfaces old ideas at the perfect intervals.

🌱 Growth mindset

Errors say "errors are data." Bugs are celebrated, not hidden.

🎮 Gentle motivation

XP, streaks, badges, confetti — rewarding mastery, not grinding.

🍳 Dual coding

Every concept pairs with a real-life analogy to stick faster.

What you can learn

How the web works, front-end (HTML/CSS/JS), back-end (Node/Express/APIs/databases), SQL, Git, JavaScript foundations, Python, dev tools, design basics (Canva/UX-UI), web safety, career path, and React basics.
