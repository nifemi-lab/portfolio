## 9. Package Justification (for "why is this more expensive?")

**BASIC — $25 — "Starter one-pager"**
The Basic package covers a single page built from scratch — layout, responsive breakpoints, a contact section and testing on phone, tablet and desktop — which is a solid chunk of focused work even when it goes smoothly. $25 is a fair entry price for hand-written code instead of a template, and it keeps the risk low for a first project together. One revision is plenty for one round of changes on one page.

**STANDARD — $60 — "Small site"**
Three pages plus a working, validated contact form and live deployment is roughly double the scope of Basic — more layout, more content to fit, and the extra work of putting the site online and testing it there. $60 still works out well below what that amount of custom work usually costs, and two revisions give you room to adjust the design once you see it live. You're paying for a finished, deployed site, not a folder of files.

**PREMIUM — $120 — "Full site"**
Six pages with a contact/quote form, basic SEO setup, deployment and 7 days of after-delivery support is a real project: planning the structure, building each page, testing across screen sizes, then being on call for a week. $120 is fair because the setup cost is spread across a whole site instead of charged page by page, and three revisions mean we can fine-tune it without surprise fees. The week of support alone usually covers the small changes people want once a site is live.
